ReWood Heat Pellets

ReWood Heat Pellets is a proposed Romanian biomass-energy business that turns clean, dry wood processing residues such as sawdust and wood chips into consistent heating pellets.

The business adresses 2 connected problems:wood processing residues have to be handled and moved, while households and smal businesses need a dependable, convenient heating fuel with predictable quality.
